# Latent representation and physical-control results

The current evidence supports the autoregressive conditional generator as the main reference. Adding a variational latent vector, increasing auxiliary supervision, or supervising the posterior mean does not establish an improvement in novel structures that meet their requested energy class. The representation still contains measurable geometric fingerprints. Those fingerprints are already present in an untrained graph encoder and occur at a scale far below the posterior sampling noise.

The initial comparison contains five fits each of AR, CVAE D1/D2/D4, cyclic-beta D2, and three descriptor-head controls at weight 1. The strength study adds weights 10 and 100 with genuine, condition-only and shuffled targets, five fits each. The final study adds posterior-mean supervision at weight 10, again with those three controls. Every fit runs4,000 updates; terminal checkpoints are primary. Validation loss continues to change, so this budget is not a convergence certificate.

The main final comparison uses new paired streams and an independently frozen common history. Its balanced novel/correct-energy-class yield is 63.333±1.758% for the mean-supervised model and 63.472±0.856% for AR under UMA; MACE gives 64.080±1.848% and 64.201±1.191%. These values are mean±sampleSD across five fits. The paired UMA mean-minus-AR effect is−0.139 percentage points, with a 95% Student-t interval half-width of 1.874 points. The genuine mean-supervised guide does not reliably increase the requested vacancy-pair descriptor: its upper-minus-lower actual response is approximately−1.0×10⁻⁵±2.3×10⁻⁵. The shuffled control can move the descriptor as much or more. Full results, including head errors and all controls, are in `mean_family_five_fit_summary.csv`; seed and cell tables remain in `analysis_oracle` and `analysis_descriptors`.

The descriptor C_s (also called C_vv) is the fraction of nearest-neighbor directed periodic edges with vacancies at both ends. It has no units. TRAIN means and standard deviations are fitted separately within each composition/measured-energy-class cell. This removes the descriptor mean already explained by the supplied condition. It is an ordering descriptor, not an energy, equilibrium distribution, or relaxed-geometry label. The nearest shell contains288 directed edges at approximately2.8113 Å in this parent.

The posthoc conditional probe corrects an important limitation of the original pooled probe: large differences between condition means can hide tiny variations within one condition. With TRAIN-only centering/scaling of μ within each cell, ridge regression with fixed α=1 recovers C_s from held-out posterior means. Balanced VAL MSE is 0.1009±0.0963 for D2 and 0.0759±0.0823 for mean supervision, versus the TRAIN-zero reference 1.0212. Shuffling the probe's TRAIN targets removes this recovery. At the predeclared illustrative condition 24 Li/middle, five random graph initializations perform even better: MSE 0.00671±0.00591. This establishes a geometric feature fingerprint; it does not establish a learned or useful stochastic latent representation. No supervised-only training campaign was needed after this control.

The encoder is not algebraically count-only. Occupancies enter nonlinear node/message layers before pooling. Read-only FP32 and FP64 forward checks distinguish fixed-count masks at random initialization and after training. Float64 accumulation is needed for moments of tiny μ variations: direct `np.std(float32)` on arrays with much larger constant offsets exaggerated some illustrative spreads. Original outputs remain preserved; use `structural_precision_correction` for corrected moments/figures. The conditional ridge's fitting/centering/scaling already used float64, so its MSE results are unchanged. These encoder diagnostics use CPU evaluation; the primary generation and MLIP comparisons retain their frozen GPU protocols.

There is a precise distinction between information in deterministic μ and information in noisy posterior samples. For the 986 VAL rows at 24 Li/middle in fit seed 2026111301, let q_i be each diagonal-Gaussian encoder posterior and q̄ their uniform mixture. Convexity gives I_VAL(mask;Z|c)=mean_i KL(q_i||q̄)≤mean_ij KL(q_i||q_j). The mean pair divergences are of order10⁻¹¹or smaller for the three illustrated encoders, while KL to the standard-Normal prior is of order10⁻³. Almost identical noisy posteriors can therefore contain negligible mask information even though their deterministic means retain a recoverable fingerprint. This is an empirical fixed-condition encoder-joint bound, not a population statement or a bound on the generated decoder joint. Matched prior interventions and actual generated descriptors/energies supply the separate generation test.

The pair formula uses a stable exponential difference, with an independent torch KL check, Decimal 80 spot checks at the observed tiny divergences, and a cancellation-free Taylor upper envelope. Use rounded orders rather than certified last digits. The Gaussian and Jensen calculations, their scope and validation are recorded in `posterior_information_bound*.json`.
