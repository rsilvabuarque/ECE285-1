All thresholds here are original H0 fixed-low/fixed-high teacher-schema thresholds, not pilot409. Novelty means selected eligible unique canonical masks divided by all128attempts in a cell. Conditional bin accuracy is in-bin hits divided by selected masks. Per-cell yield is exactly novelty times conditional accuracy. The symmetric numerical decomposition is deltaY = deltaNovelty * meanAccuracy + deltaAccuracy * meanNovelty; cellwise contributions are averaged over all nine cells where both accuracies exist, with pooled fallback explicitly flagged for unscored cells. It is an algebraic decomposition, not a causal explanation of why either rate changed. Sparse classes alone do not establish a failure mechanism; R1 range was positive. Actual TRAIN support/extrema/edge motion/loss-weight concentration and moving-bin UMA fidelity are separate observations. Existing raw tables and checks are unchanged. Sample SD is across three paired fit/draw replicates, not a confidence interval.
