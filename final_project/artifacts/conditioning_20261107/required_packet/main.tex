{\Large\bfseries Conditional generation of cathode vacancy patterns\\
at requested compositions and energy ranges}\par
\vspace{4pt}
Ricardo Silva Buarque, Beihao Gu, and Varad Kottawar\\
ECE 285 midterm draft --- November 9, 2026

\begin{abstract}
We study whether conditional variational autoencoders (CVAEs) efficiently propose
new Li/vacancy arrangements at specified compositions and energy ranges in a
fixed layered LiCoO$_2$ parent. Exact-count decoding enforces composition, while
energy control must be learned and independently measured. Earlier binary and
multibin models enriched low energies relative to uniform sampling but lost to
frozen low-energy-seed mutation. The extension separates training-policy,
latent-size, symmetry, and physical-neighbor effects, and audits whether deletion
energies represent relaxed ordering. Causal decoder neighborhoods improve final
requested-bin yield over frozen mutation under UMA and MACE, while latent-use
diagnostics limit attribution to the variational pathway. Teacher-defined success
is distinguished from DFT accuracy and relaxed geometry generation.
\end{abstract}

\section{Physical motivation, revised question, and data}
Direct cathode recycling seeks to restore useful composition and structure;
hydrothermal treatment followed by annealing can regenerate degraded LiCoO$_2$
(LCO) \cite{shi2018}. Our longer-term application is to select informative
structures for reference calculations and better lithium-replenishment models.
The class experiment studies conditional generation, rather than simulating the
recycling liquid or reactor. Li/vacancy ordering changes energies at a fixed Li
content \cite{vanderVen1998}, creating a large discrete search space.

Density functional theory (DFT) supplies an approximate electronic-structure
reference at specified species, coordinates, and cell. A machine-learned
interatomic potential (MLIP) approximates the energy and its force/cell
derivatives. A single-point call keeps coordinates fixed; relaxation moves them
toward a local minimum. Molecular dynamics (MD) uses forces to propagate atoms.
Drawing a vacancy pattern performs none of these physical operations.

The parent is Li$_{48}$Co$_{48}$O$_{96}$; retaining $n$ Li gives $N_a=144+n$
present atoms and fraction $x=n/48$. The generated object is an occupation vector
$o\in\{0,1\}^{48}$, with $o_j=1$ for Li present and vacancy $v_j=1-o_j$.
Li indices are full-parent rows 48--95, zero based. Primary counts are
$n=12,24,36$. The main labels are energies of pristine-derived deletions, not
relaxed minima. UMA-S-1.2.1/\texttt{omat}, FP32 with TF32 off, defines the primary
new-label teacher; matched MACE checks use a separate energy namespace.

\subsection{Independent examples and protected roles}
The parent's 192 retained permutations define an orbit: all related occupation
patterns under this fixed host's symmetries. Its canonical key is the smallest
transformed vacancy string. Grouping precedes splitting; augmentation does not
create independent examples. At half filling, 32,247,603,683,100 raw masks give
168,025,313,938 orbits for this cell/group, not for every supercell shape.
The frozen round-0 data have 40,157 training, 4,967 validation, and 5,123 test
orbits. New development evidence selects policy; a fresh final protocol/history
is frozen before proposals. Previously inspected assessments cannot be renamed
untouched tests. No new DFT or MLIP fine-tuning campaign is run.

A Pt--Ni study already combines occupation-based CVAEs, property classes, and
UMA evaluation \cite{wakamiya2026}. Our contribution is an interpretable,
symmetry-aware test against strong composition-preserving mutation, including
negative results. The software interface describes a general parent/mobile-ion
sublattice, but present physical evidence is LCO-specific.

\section{Conditional model, exact count, and physical graph}
At each composition, training-only energies $e_i=E_i/N_a$ (eV/atom) define nearest-
rank $q_{20}$ and $q_{80}$ boundaries. Low is $e\le q_{20}$, middle is
$q_{20}<e\le q_{80}$, and high is $e>q_{80}$. Ties stay together; unsupported
joint requests fail explicitly. Each schema records parent, teacher/head,
precision, units, geometry protocol, boundaries, support, and input hashes.

The encoder $q_\phi(z\mid o,c)$ predicts a Gaussian mean $\mu$ and diagonal
variance $\sigma^2$. A GRU decoder of hidden size 64 defines
\begin{equation}
p_\theta(o\mid z,c)=\prod_{j=1}^{48}p_\theta(o_j\mid o_{<j},z,c),
\qquad z\sim\mathcal N(0,I)\ \text{at generation}.
\end{equation}
The decoder receives conditions and latent context at every step. During fitting,
previous true bits provide teacher forcing; during generation they are sampled.
If the remaining count forces a bit, its conditional probability is one and its
reconstruction penalty zero. Energy membership has no such hard guarantee.

The fitted objective is
\begin{equation}
\mathcal L_\beta=\mathbb E_{q_\phi}[-\log p_\theta(o\mid z,c)]
+\frac{\beta}{2}\sum_{k=1}^{D}
\left(\mu_k^2+\sigma_k^2-\log\sigma_k^2-1\right).
\end{equation}
The first term reconstructs masks; the second is KL divergence to the sampling
prior. $D$ is latent dimension and $\beta$ its dimensionless regularization
weight. Training $\beta$ warms to 0.1; alternative checkpoint criteria use
validation weights 1 and 0.1 on the \emph{same trajectory}. The KL sum is not
silently divided by $D$. Neither good reconstruction nor large KL establishes
useful prior samples \cite{kingma2014,sohn2015,alemi2018}.
Class balancing changes training-only row weights while preserving their total;
the same weights average reconstruction and summed KL. Validation retains base
weights and fixed two-draw latent noise, independent of training randomness.

\subsection{What spatial feature extraction changes}
A reusable parent schema stores host species, all possible mobile sites,
positions/cell in \AA, periodic directions, valid symmetries, and graph edges
with integer image shifts. Vacant sites remain nodes. Distances come from the
crystal, not adjacent sequence indices. Message passing learns combinations of
neighbor features and pools them into latent parameters \cite{gilmer2017}.
Backpropagation differentiates the CVAE loss through these operations; it does
not minimize a physical energy. An encoder-only graph is absent during prior
generation. Decoder neighborhood summaries therefore use only already assigned
occupancies and an explicit unassigned state. Future target bits or full-mask
encoder embeddings must not bypass the latent channel. An indexed GRU with graph
features is not automatically permutation/rotation equivariant. The context
comparison adds decoder summaries to the same graph-encoder control; an initial
fit was replicated at two more seeds before the fresh final assessment.

\section{Experiments, observables, and scientific controls}
The full protocol, commands, selected configurations, and per-experiment status
are supplied in the portable packet. Historical and new assessments retain
different scope labels. The new design first compares class balancing off/on
and both checkpoint rules at a fixed update budget with three fit seeds.
Matched latent dimensions 4, 8, 16, 32 reuse the selected dimension 8 trajectory;
a no-latent AR control isolates the latent pathway. A canonical-versus-training-
augmentation comparison precedes graph comparisons, so orientation and graph
effects are not silently combined. Parameter counts and actual updates are saved.

Latent diagnostics include summed/per-dimension KL, encoded-mean variance across
configurations, and prior-sample usefulness. ``Active'' components use declared
mean-variance cutoffs with sensitivity reported. These are descriptive
diagnostics, not a theorem of collapse or a physical convergence test
\cite{dieng2019,ichikawa2024}. The latter paper analyzes a simplified linear VAE,
not this crystal network. Posterior reconstructions and prior generation answer
different questions. Paired low/middle/high requests share latent vectors and
AR random streams; separate changes of $z$ test latent sensitivity.

\subsection{Baselines and complete accounting}
Uniform sampling is uniform over raw fixed-count masks. Frozen mutation selects
a protected training seed uniformly in the requested bin, selects one/two/three
swaps uniformly, then selects distinct occupied removals and vacant insertions
uniformly. It has no distance restriction, energy acceptance, or online population
update. Every proposal is frozen before scoring. Method names containing
``adapt'' can identify the source seed dataset and do not imply adaptive search.
An edit model must receive the same seeds and swap distribution as mutation;
joint seed/child transformations preserve edit alignment.

For requested bin $b$, the primary useful yield is
\begin{equation}
Y_b=\frac{\#\{\text{distinct novel orbits measured in requested bin }b\}}
{\#\{\text{all attempts requesting }b\}}.
\end{equation}
Known masks, repeats, invalid outputs, and failures remain attempts. Report
novelty to training and to all frozen history separately, confusion by composition
and fit seed, energy distributions, distances from training/seeds, and useful
energy versus attempts and actual new scoring calls. Fixed-original-$q_{20}$
yield is a separate requested-low metric. Fit seeds are independent model
replicates; latent draws are not. Teacher agreement is not independent DFT truth.

\subsection{Two audits before interpreting new training}
The energy-target audit pairs existing deletion and fixed-cell endpoint energies
for the same masks, retaining failures and protected lineages. It tests rank and
low-tail changes, Li-site reassignment, and endpoint occupation-class merges.
Occupation classes do not certify unique potential-energy basins. A second audit
uses a symmetry-invariant structural descriptor: the nearest-shell fraction of
eligible mobile-site pairs that are both vacant. Pairs are consistently counted
once; vacancy is $1-o_j$. Train-derived descriptor ranges and joint support are
checked before any joint-condition pilot. Mutation receives the same request.

Static temperature acquisition remains secondary. Its exponent uses total-cell
energy gaps, with orbit multiplicity for exhaustive representatives or retained
raw draw frequencies for a raw-mask reference, never both. Proposal-biased
training histograms and latent probabilities do not measure physical entropy.
The prior reference has low effective sample size and identical coarse request
allocations across temperatures; those limitations are retained.

\subsection{Physical validation and generalization boundary}
The archived matched DFT audit remains informative but cohort dependent: UMA's
median rank correlation is 0.97366 over seven historical O3 rattled pools and
0.73177 over ten Hu O3 optimized pools. At Li$_{10}$Co$_{12}$O$_{24}$, it selects
a structure ranked last by DFT in the complete five-member pool. High correlation
on displaced frames does not guarantee ordering of nearly degenerate minima.
Historical/Hu DFT parameters differ, and their absolute energies are not pooled.
Good ranks do not certify forces, saddle points, or thermal weights.

No result establishes global minima, physical entropy, reactor optima, downstream
MLIP improvement, or zero-shot material/size transfer. A generic parent interface
and synthetic alternate-parent checks establish applicability of code, not accuracy
on new chemistry. A structure can have many Li hops: a barrier label requires
an occupied source, empty destination, periodic displacement, and a consistent
geometry/teacher protocol. Any later MLIP NEB pilot is a feasibility study, not
barrier-conditioned CVAE training or a DFT-validated diffusivity. Extensive
ablations and optional extensions remain linked supplements to this report.
