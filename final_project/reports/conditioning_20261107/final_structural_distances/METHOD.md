For occupancy vectors a,b, d_G(a,b)=min_g sum_j |a_j-b_{g^-1(j)}|, over all192 actual retained parent operations. The comparison uses all periodic parent translations/point symmetries; it is not Hamming distance between canonical representatives. For fixed TRAIN reference R, d_R(a)=min_{b in R}d_G(a,b). Since R is a subset of TRAIN, d_R(a)>=d_TRAIN(a). Thus this is an upper bound, not a certified distance from the full training set. No positive lower bound on full-TRAIN distance follows. References are1024 deterministic SHA-ranked canonical TRAIN orbits/count. Units are differing mobile-site occupations, not angstrom. For equal counts, Hamming/2 is the minimum number of simultaneous removal/insertion swaps under that alignment. Mutation raw seeds/children retain their original joint orientation, and their raw distance must be exactly2k; its orbit minimum may be smaller, including zero. All attempts, including repeated orbits, remain in tables and averages. Within each fit seed, summaries weight each composition/bin cell equally. No energy labels, MLIP calls, new structures, optimization, novelty reclassification, or model selection were used. The reference identities were frozen before distance computation; the already fixed final proposal pool and assessment decision remain unchanged.
