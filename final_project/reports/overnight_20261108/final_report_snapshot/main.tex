{\Large\bfseries Conditional generation of cathode vacancy patterns:\\
separating latent learning, spatial decoding, and relaxed energy}\par\vspace{4 pt}
Ricardo Silva Buarque, Beihao Gu, and Varad Kottawar\\
ECE 285 midterm --- November 8, 2026
\end{center}
\begin{abstract}
We test whether a conditional variational autoencoder (CVAE) proposes new
Li/vacancy configurations at a requested composition and energy range more
efficiently than uniform sampling and mutation. Earlier spatial decoding improved
teacher-defined yield, but its latent variables were nearly unused. The present
matched controls separate a graph-posterior CVAE, a no-latent autoregressive
sampler, and a dense-posterior CVAE with the same causal spatial decoder.
Thirty-seven 4,000-update fits are complete. Development evidence shows spatial AR matches
the graph CVAE, while a dense posterior retains information without useful
prior-generation gain. On frozen FINAL proposals, radial-shell AR achieves
63.04\% UMA balanced novel in-bin yield, versus 62.20\% for its CVAE and
58.37\% for mutation. A separate 6,000-mask cohort compares deletion and
relaxed-endpoint targets; endpoint-label training does not improve endpoint
LOW yield in the matched test. Exhaustive small-cell
enumeration quantifies approximate symmetry and teacher disagreement. No new DFT
or interatomic-potential fine-tuning is performed, and generative, numerical, and
physical validation remain distinct.
\end{abstract}

\section{Motivation, revised question, and independent data}
Direct recycling aims to restore useful cathode composition and structure rather
than dissolve the material into its constituent elements. Hydrothermal processing
and annealing have regenerated spent LiCoO$_2$ (LCO) experimentally
\cite{shi2018}. Our longer-term motivation is to select informative atomistic
configurations for reference calculations and improved recycling models.
The class project studies conditional generation of vacancy patterns; it does not
simulate a hydrothermal liquid or establish optimal reactor conditions.

Density functional theory (DFT) supplies an approximate electronic-structure
reference at specified atoms, positions, and cell. A machine-learned interatomic
potential (MLIP) predicts potential energy and its force/cell derivatives. A
single-point prediction leaves geometry unchanged. Relaxation moves atoms toward
a local minimum; molecular dynamics (MD) uses forces to propagate them in time.
A sampled vacancy mask performs none of these operations automatically.

Li/vacancy ordering changes energy even at fixed composition
\cite{vanderVen1998}. The working parent is Li$_{48}$Co$_{48}$O$_{96}$.
An occupation vector $o\in\{0,1\}^{48}$ has $o_j=1$ for a present Li at parent
site $j$ and $o_j=0$ for a vacancy. Its count is $n=\sum_j o_j$; the material
contains $N_a=144+n$ atoms and Li fraction $x=n/48$. Primary counts are
$n=12,24,36$. The parent geometry and cell are fixed for deletion labels.
The primary teacher is UMA-S-1.2.1/\texttt{omat}; the independent teacher check is
MACE-MH-1/\texttt{omat\_pbe}. Both use FP32, TF32 off, with checkpoint and
inference receipts. A missing precision field in an old condition schema is not
evidence that its predictions used another precision.

The parent's 192 retained site permutations define an \emph{orbit}: all vacancy
masks related by those operations. The lexicographically smallest transformed
vacancy string identifies the orbit. Grouping precedes splitting; symmetry
augmentation supplies equivalent views, not additional independent examples.
The fixed round-0 dataset contains 40,157 TRAIN, 4,967 VAL and 5,123 TEST orbits.
Only TRAIN supplies fit examples and class thresholds; VAL selects checkpoints.
TEST labels are not used for these decisions. Before FINAL generation, a history
of 344,001 known orbits was frozen, including development proposals, acquired
structures and observed research trajectories. Novelty uses this complete history.

The revised scientific question is whether an improvement is due to the CVAE
latent pathway, to causal spatial autoregression, or to their combination, and
whether a deletion-energy advantage survives actual geometry relaxation.
This is more discriminating than comparing a CVAE only against uniform masks.

\section{Model, conditions, and controlled alternatives}
The condition is $c=(n/48,b)$, with energy class $b\in\{\mathrm{low},\mathrm{middle},
\mathrm{high}\}$. Within each count, TRAIN energy quantiles $q_{20},q_{80}$ define
the frozen class boundaries. Energy is in eV per present atom for class
assignment; the teacher, parent, geometry convention and thresholds belong to
one condition schema. A low request is a supported energy \emph{range}, not a
guarantee of a global minimum or an energy-gradient optimization.

An encoder produces a diagonal Gaussian
$q_\phi(z\mid o,c)=\mathcal N(\mu_\phi,\operatorname{diag}\sigma_\phi^2)$,
where $z\in\mathbb R^4$ is the latent vector. The reparameterization
$z=\mu+\sigma\odot\epsilon$, $\epsilon\sim\mathcal N(0,I)$, permits stochastic
training. At generation, a fresh $z\sim\mathcal N(0,I)$ is used without a target
mask. The conditional autoregressive decoder factors
\begin{equation}
 p_\theta(o\mid z,c)=\prod_{i=1}^{48}
 p_\theta(o_i\mid o_{<i},z,c,h_i).
\end{equation}
Here $o_{<i}$ is the already assigned prefix, and $h_i$ summarizes local
occupied, vacant and unassigned sites. At training, the true prefix is supplied
(teacher forcing); at generation, earlier sampled decisions form the prefix.
The future target occupations are excluded from $h_i$. If the remaining required
Li count is zero, later sites are vacant; if it equals the remaining site count,
all are occupied. This enforces $\sum o_i=n$ exactly.

For one training row, the loss is
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal L(o,c)=-\mathbb E_{q_\phi}\sum_i\log p_\theta(o_i\mid o_{<i},z,c,h_i)
 +\beta_t D_{\mathrm{KL}}\!\left(q_\phi(z\mid o,c)\Vert\mathcal N(0,I)\right).
\end{equation}
The first term rewards mask reconstruction; the KL term regularizes the
posterior toward the sampling prior. Both are measured in nats with natural
logarithms. The same row weights multiply reconstruction and the sum of KL over
latent components. Class balancing changes TRAIN weights only; VAL retains its
base weights and fixed Monte Carlo noise. The block-update KL warm-up
and selection objective with $\beta=0.1$ are matched across controls. All full
fits use 4,000 updates: 37 trajectories total, including bounded screens and six
paired-target fits, for 148,000 updates.

The graph encoder passes messages on the full parent, including host species
and vacant mobile sites, then pools host/mobile features \cite{gilmer2017}.
The causal decoder uses only prefix-derived features on that fixed graph.
Its six-feature baseline is compared with nine occupied/vacant/unassigned
fractions in three radial shells, including interlayer neighbors. Three-fit
development confirmation retained A-shell as the best genuine CVAE point
estimate and B-shell as the strongest AR; A-shell's improvement over A was
unresolved across fits. A/B and their shell variants have five FINAL replicates;
C/D/remaining-count B have three. Replicates 0804/05 were FINAL-only.
This is not a coordinate-generating equivariant model. The software accepts a
different parent/mobile ion, but a new material or cell size has not been validated.

KL, per-component $\operatorname{Var}(\mu_j)$, resampling $z$ with shared decoder
uniforms, and changing $b$ with shared $z$/uniforms test different properties.
A small reconstruction loss alone does not establish useful latent codes
\cite{alemi2018}. A cyclic KL policy is a future controlled candidate
\cite{fu2019}, not an assumed cure for collapse or evidence of better sampling.

\section{Evaluation and mechanistic evidence}
Uniform masks select exactly $n$ sites. Mutation starts from a frozen TRAIN seed
in the requested bin and swaps equal numbers of present/vacant sites. Seed
information, proposal counts and swap budgets are declared. Neither baseline
is credited with a new unique structure when it merely repeats one already
seen. Every attempted proposal remains in the denominator, including duplicates,
unsupported/failed requests and invalid outputs.

For $A_{snb}$ attempted proposals from replicate $s$, count $n$ and requested bin
$b$, the primary score is
\begin{equation}
Y_{snb}=\frac{\#\{\text{distinct valid orbits, new to frozen history, teacher bin}=b\}}
{A_{snb}}.
\end{equation}
The balanced yield averages all nine count/bin cells equally and is then
reported per fitted replicate. The historical low-energy score using its original
round-0 $q_{20}$ remains a separately named metric. Best energy versus attempts
and versus actual new teacher queries are secondary search metrics; they need
not agree with requested-bin yield. Shared queries are deduplicated physically
without changing attempted-proposal denominators.

Paired condition-response tests hold $z$ and decoder uniforms fixed while changing
the requested energy class; independent MLIP rescoring determines the realized
energy shift. Latent-response tests change $z$ while fixing condition/uniforms.
Paired probes report raw Hamming differences and orbit equality, failures,
novelty, energy distributions and composition-resolved results. FINAL structural
distance minimizes over all 192 operations against 1,024 fixed TRAIN orbits/count;
it is an upper bound on full-TRAIN nearest distance. A few fit seeds give
descriptive variation, not a calibrated confidence interval over all future fits.

class yields. The FINAL loss and nonzero fit spread are retained. Novelty
uses the complete frozen history. FINAL outcomes do not select or modify models.
Teacher agreement is robustness evidence, not calibrated uncertainty or DFT
validation.

\section{Does deletion-energy control survive relaxation?}
For the same initial mask $o$, define $E_{\mathrm{del}}(o)$ at literal parent
coordinates and $E_{\mathrm{end}}(o)$ after fixed-cell atomic relaxation.
The latter is a reached local endpoint, not a certified global minimum. Two
models have identical input masks, splits, architecture, initialization
policy and update budget, but distinct TRAIN-fitted condition thresholds for
these two energy targets. Both generators must be evaluated under \emph{both}
energy definitions. A mask generator conditioned on endpoint energy still
outputs a pristine-derived deletion geometry until relaxation is actually run.

The frozen 6,000-mask cohort has 1,200 TRAIN, 400 VAL and 400 TEST masks/count.
UMA fixed-cell FIRE uses maximum force $0.05$ eV/\AA, at most 500 steps and a
120-s attempt cap; coordinates and atom identities are retained. Of 6,000
attempts, 5,992 converged and eight were interrupted. The shared fit set has
3,594 TRAIN and 1,200 VAL pairs. Per-count TRAIN LOW support is 240; endpoint
VAL LOW support is 77/81/85. This passes the predeclared minimum support of
30/count/role, an operational heuristic rather than a power calculation.
TEST was sealed until the final scoring gate; 1,198/1,200 TEST pairs are known,
with both failures retained and no threshold refitting.

At $n=12/24/36$, independent TEST deletion--endpoint Spearman rank correlation
is 0.926/0.966/0.907. Of deletion-LOW masks, 74.7/82.6/77.6\% also reach
endpoint LOW; they recover 73.8/85.1/78.6\% of endpoint-LOW masks. Thus deletion
energy is a useful but imperfect proxy. Mean relaxation lowers energy by
30.52/20.84/11.91 meV/atom. A high correlation alone cannot establish a
generator's endpoint low-tail enrichment.

\section{Interpretation, limits, and independent completion tasks}
The principal mechanistic distinction is between \emph{condition control} and
\emph{latent use}. A decoder may respond well to a requested energy bin while
effectively ignoring $z$. Conversely, an encoder can reconstruct a training
mask through an informative posterior yet generate poorly from its prior.
The present A/B/C controls directly separate these possibilities. Retaining a
true CVAE is useful only if its measured behavior supports the latent pathway;
an AR result that matches or wins remains scientifically informative.
At this data/update budget, B-shell has the best balanced point estimate under
both teachers, while a latent-specific advantage is unestablished. The current
learned samplers enrich the endpoint LOW tail, but matched endpoint-label
training does not improve it over deletion-label training. These observations
support spatial conditional sampling without claiming that CVAEs or endpoint
labels cannot help under another controlled design.

Pristine-derived deletions omit relaxation, local strain, magnetic degrees of
freedom and competing host structures. Endpoint labels address one of these
limits under a stated MLIP and fixed cell, without proving a ground state or
validated chemistry. Class conditions are empirical ranges, not thermodynamic
populations. The exact small-cell probabilities provide a finite-model reference
for raw reference-pool error, not a justification for treating a generated
proposal histogram as an equilibrium distribution.

The separate research supplement reports completed bounded checks. Exhaustive
12-site enumeration scores all 1,364 raw masks plus repeat controls under both
teachers. Approximate symmetry has measurable energy spread, so every raw mask
is retained. Finite-model configurational entropies differ between teachers;
they are not experimental LCO thermodynamics or evidence of network size
transfer. A null test using the actual 537-mode harmonic spectrum exposes
finite-window bias and spurious fluidicity in thermal estimators, not an MD
measurement of anharmonicity.

For one saved migration event, fixed-endpoint image/tolerance checks pass a
restricted 0.02-eV path-span target, but changing endpoint relaxation changes
the span by 0.0264 eV. Two finite-difference steps find one stable negative
internal mode at a candidate saddle. Its downhill branches do not establish the
full marked endpoint-to-endpoint hop; one reaches an off-site low-force state
with the same assigned vacancy mask. Another route fails its step cap.
Consequently, no stable DFT-validated hop barrier, finite-temperature activation
free energy or diffusivity is claimed. Exact-coordinate viewing files and atom
mappings support later review. These extensions do not substitute for the
class-model comparisons.
