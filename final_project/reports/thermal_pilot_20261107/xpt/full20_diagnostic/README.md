# Full 20 ps: trajectory diagnostic, not initial-basin free energies

Two of the four trajectories leave their starting occupation assignments at
16.702 and 16.882 ps and remain near different Li sites. All raw observations
and mechanically computed estimator fields are preserved. Labels such as
`basin_1` identify the **starting minimum**, not a claim that the full trajectory
remained in its basin. Any computed difference or harmonic-reference column in
these tables consequently cannot establish that initial basin's vibrational
free energy or population.

Use `full20_assignment_event_scoped.pdf/png` for the correctly annotated spectra
and the independent event audit under `../../numerics/`. Generic automatically
generated free-energy figures are retained locally as execution evidence and
excluded from publication. The first-10-ps analysis is a separately labeled,
posthoc restricted-window diagnostic; it does not retroactively make these
full trajectories basin-restricted equilibrium samples.
