{\Large\bfseries Conditional generation of cathode vacancy patterns\\
at requested compositions and energy ranges}\par
\vspace{4pt}
Ricardo Silva Buarque, Beihao Gu, and Varad Kottawar\\
ECE 285 midterm draft --- November 9, 2026
\end{center}
\begin{abstract}
We extend an existing energy-conditioned autoregressive (AR) conditional variational
autoencoder (CVAE) to request low, middle, or high energy Li/vacancy patterns in
one fixed layered LiCoO$_2$ (O3) parent. A separate acquisition policy allocates requests using
static machine-learned interatomic-potential (MLIP) energies at relevant
operating temperatures. Under UMA, novel in-bin yield is 31.0\% for multibin, 33.0\% for uniform sampling
and 58.4\% for bin-seeded mutation: the extension does not improve control.
These are teacher-defined generation and acquisition tests,
not measurements of physical configurational entropy or reactor equilibrium.
\end{abstract}

\section{Physical motivation and revised objective}
Direct cathode recycling seeks to recover useful composition and structure.
Hydrothermal treatment and subsequent annealing have regenerated degraded
LiCoO$_2$ (LCO) \cite{shi2018}. Our longer-term application is to select
informative reference structures for better models of lithium replenishment.
The present class problem is controlled generation, rather than a simulation of
the recycling liquid or reaction pathway.

Density functional theory (DFT) computes an electronic-structure reference at
specified atoms and cell under a stated approximation and numerical protocol.
An MLIP approximates the energy and its force/cell derivatives cheaply.
Molecular dynamics (MD) propagates atoms with those forces; appropriately sampled
trajectories can estimate thermal observables. A single-point call fixes
coordinates; a relaxation moves them toward a local minimum. Drawing a vacancy
mask does neither operation.

Li/vacancy ordering can change energies at a fixed lithium content, as established
in first-principles LCO studies \cite{vanderVen1998}. Foundation datasets have
broad equilibrium and displaced-structure coverage, but do not guarantee the
needed defect/order coverage; OMat24 discusses these limitations explicitly
\cite{omat2026}. We do not assume all teacher errors are constant offsets or
that every foundation model was trained only on pristine LCO.

\section{Parent, geometry protocol, and symmetry}
The main parent is Li$_{48}$Co$_{48}$O$_{96}$. Retaining $n$ Li gives
$N_a=144+n$ present atoms and dimensionless Li fraction $x=n/48$.
Occupancy $o_j=1$ means parent Li site $j$ is occupied; vacancy $v_j=1-o_j$ means
removed. Li are full-parent atom rows 48--95 (zero based), not the first rows.
The controlled comparison uses $n=12,24,36$. Labels refer to unchanged,
pristine-derived deletion coordinates, not relaxed minima.

The parent's 192 retained site permutations identify equivalent masks. Their
orbit is the set of distinct related raw occupation patterns; its canonical key
is the smallest transformed vacancy string. Grouping precedes splitting and
augmentation; all frames/teacher labels of a lineage stay together. At $n=24$,
Burnside counting gives 168,025,313,938 orbits for this group, compared with
32,247,603,683,100 raw masks. This is a count for the specified cell, not all
48-site cell shapes or host phases. O1/spinel transitions and size/material
transfer are outside this fixed-parent generator.

\section{Composition-and-energy-range CVAE}
The model already used composition and a binary low-energy class. The new schema
extends this condition to three energy ranges. At each composition, define
$e_i=E_i/N_a$ in eV/atom, where $E_i$ is total cell energy in eV. Sort the $m$
\emph{training} energies; the one-based element at $\lceil pm\rceil$ defines
$q_p$. The requested bin $b$ is
\begin{equation}
b(e)=\begin{cases}
\mathrm{low},&e\le q_{0.2},\\
\mathrm{middle},&q_{0.2}<e\le q_{0.8},\\
\mathrm{high},&e>q_{0.8}.
\end{cases}
\end{equation}
Exact ties stay together. Empty bins are explicitly unsupported; sparse
compositions are not forced to have three classes. The versioned schema stores
edges, support, units, teacher/head, geometry protocol, parent/data/split hashes,
and content identity. It is shared by fitting, generation, allocation and
assessment. It uses no validation or assessment energies to fit the boundaries.

The condition is $c=[x,\mathrm{onehot}(b)]$, with four components. Historical
binary $[x,\mathbf{1}_{\rm low}]$ and composition-only $[x]$ controls remain
functional with their original input dimensions. Binary middle/high requests
explicitly map to its rest class. Incompatible checkpoint shapes are rejected;
energy conditioning itself is not a new invention here.

The encoder $q_\phi(z\mid o,c)$ predicts the mean and diagonal variance of an
eight-dimensional Gaussian. At generation, $z\sim\mathcal N(0,I)$ and $c$ enter
a gated recurrent unit (GRU) decoder with hidden size 64. Its normalized autoregressive likelihood is
\begin{equation}
p_\theta(o\mid z,c)=\prod_{j=1}^{48}p_\theta(o_j\mid o_{<j},z,c).
\end{equation}
Site index $j$ is a sequence position, not MD time. Observed preceding bits are
used during fitting (teacher forcing); sampled bits are fed back at generation.
When the remaining Li count forces a zero or one, that choice has probability
one and zero reconstruction penalty. Thus every valid sample has exactly $n$ Li.
The chain-rule construction is related to variational autoregressive approaches
\cite{wu2019}, but our supervised conditional objective differs.

The loss is
\begin{equation}
\mathcal L_\beta=\mathbb E_{q_\phi}[-\log p_\theta(o\mid z,c)]
+\beta D_{\rm KL}\!\left[q_\phi(z\mid o,c)\Vert\mathcal N(0,I)\right].
\end{equation}
Parameters $\phi,\theta$ belong to encoder and decoder; $I$ is the identity
matrix, and $\mathbb E$ denotes expectation. KL measures the encoded Gaussian's
difference from the prior, with weight $\beta$. At $\beta=1$, this is a negative
conditional evidence lower bound; $\beta=0.1$ is a different regularized objective
\cite{kingma2014,sohn2015}. Good reconstruction or large KL alone does not prove
good prior samples \cite{alemi2018}. Requesting low energy does not impose an
energy inequality or provide a minimization gradient.

\subsection{Data, controls, and assessment}
The primary expanded round-0 set contains 50,247 orbit masks: 40,157 training,
4,967 validation and 5,123 test rows in protected splits. New fit configurations record
actual seeds, update/stopping limits and checkpoint selection. Previously
assessed data promoted to training become development evidence. A fresh
assessment boundary and prior-history cache are frozen before new proposals.

Controls are binary/composition CVAEs, uniform raw fixed-count sampling,
bin-seeded mutations with one to three Li/vacancy swaps, and the low-bin mutation baseline.
Mutation seeds come from the requested training bin; available information and
attempt/oracle budgets are identified. Rejected filtered draws, duplicates and
failed requests remain in accounting. Requested and measured bins, physical
$T$, policy, teacher, schema, fit seed and sampling seed stay separate. We report
confusion/hits, exact count, uniqueness, novelty to training and prior history,
and distinct novel in-bin yield per all attempts. The original fixed $q_{20}$
yield is compared only within requested-low attempts, not pooled with middle/high
requests; teacher agreement is not DFT truth.

\section{Temperature-dependent static acquisition}
The outer policy decides how often to request each bin. Physical temperature
$T$ in kelvin does not alter the latent normal prior or introduce a decoder
softmax temperature. Confirmed reactor targets motivate
$T\in\{393.15,433.15,473.15,493.15\}$ K; no calibrated pressure dependence or
hydrothermal occupation population is assumed.

For a complete list of symmetry representatives at one composition, let $i$
index a representative, $g_i$ its number of distinct raw occupancy patterns,
and $E_{\rm ref}$ a common reference energy. Define
\begin{equation}
a_i(T)=\exp\!\left[-\frac{E_i-E_{\rm ref}}{k_B T}\right],\qquad
w_i=g_i a_i,\qquad
W_b(T)=\frac{\sum_{i:b_i=b}w_i}{\sum_iw_i}.
\end{equation}
Here $k_B=8.617333262145\times10^{-5}$ eV/K; $E_i,E_{\rm ref}$ are total eV per
same-composition cell. The factors $a_i,w_i$ and bin fraction $W_b$ are
dimensionless. Stable logarithmic normalization avoids underflow. A common
energy offset cancels; distorted gaps do not. Multiplicity counts physical
occupation masks, not permutations of identical atom labels.

For a prespecified independent uniform \emph{raw-mask} reference, repeated orbit
identities are collapsed for energy calls but retain their raw sampling frequency
$f_i$. Then $w_i=f_i a_i$; multiplying by $g_i$ again would count degeneracy twice.
The entire prespecified pool is scored, including exact-geometry cache coverage;
using only convenient cache hits would bias the reference. Proposal-biased
training or CVAE histograms are not a density of states.

The exhaustive Hu 12-site reference tests the measure: its 87 representatives
cover every Li count, and orbit multiplicities sum to $\binom{12}{n}$ at each
count. At $n=6$, 17 representatives cover all 924 raw masks. Direct enumeration
checks the numerical weighted sum. This small reference is not evidence for
large-data generation or size transfer. For the 48-site reference, finite pool
size, missing rare states and concentration remain limitations.

\subsection{Concentration, mixtures, and exploration}
With $Z=\sum_iw_i$ and $p_i=w_i/Z$, representative effective sample size (ESS)
is $1/\sum_i p_i^2$. It describes concentration over representatives. A distinct
quantity,
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{ESS}_{\rm particle}=\frac{Z^2}{\sum_i m_i a_i^2},
\end{equation}
uses $m_i=f_i$ for sampled raw draws, or $m_i=g_i$ for complete raw microstates.
For equal energies it equals the number of raw draws/microstates. It is not a
confidence interval. We also inspect the maximum weight and nested-prefix
pool-size sensitivity; correlated prefixes are not independent repeats.

Both pure weights and an exploration policy are saved:
\begin{equation}
P_b(T)=(1-\epsilon)W_b(T)+\frac{\epsilon}{K},\qquad\epsilon=0.1,
\end{equation}
where $K$ is the number of supported bins. The floor is a chosen acquisition
setting, not a thermodynamic constant. Chosen temperature priorities
$\alpha_t\ge0$, $\sum_t\alpha_t=1$, define mixtures. Largest-remainder rounding
allocates exactly the declared attempt budget. Per-temperature and joint-mixture
budgets are distinguished in the plan and every attempt record.

\subsection{Why units and measured outcomes matter}
At Li$_{24}$, $N_a=168$. An illustrative $1$ meV/atom gap is $0.168$ eV/cell.
At 433.15 K, $k_BT\simeq0.03733$ eV and its equal-multiplicity relative weight is
approximately 0.0111. Incorrectly using $0.001$ eV gives approximately 0.974.
Large-cell static weights can legitimately concentrate on very few reference
samples; an exploration floor does not repair their physical accuracy.

Finally, teacher evaluations determine the generated masks' \emph{actual} bins.
Correct request totals do not establish conditional control or correct
within-bin sampling. Because the current labels are deletion energies, this is
\emph{temperature-dependent acquisition under a static MLIP surrogate}. It is
not a relaxed-basin free-energy calculation or measured configurational entropy.

\section{Historical context, interpretation, and remaining checks}
The completed November 6 binary experiment used three CVAE fit seeds and three
random replicates for sampler controls, three Li counts, and 512 attempts per group. Its original teacher-specific low-tail thresholds
and novelty boundary differ from a later assessment and are preserved as
historical evidence. Yield counts distinct novel below-threshold orbits per all
attempts, including repeats:
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{lrr}
\toprule
Historical method & UMA yield (\%) & MACE yield (\%)\\
\midrule
Uniform fixed count &18.99&19.75\\
AR CVAE, composition only &31.88&35.76\\
AR CVAE, binary energy &35.57&40.06\\
Additional adaptive AR, $\beta=0.1$ &33.81&38.91\\
Strongest mutation seed pool &52.65&54.08\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The binary CVAE beat random sampling but not strong mutation. Additional
adaptation coupled data, thresholds and class weights, so its decline does not
isolate a cause. A separate geometry CVAE reduced the matched optimizer's median
steps from 40 to 24, but a deterministic regressor also reached 24; no
CVAE-specific geometry advantage was established. These are secondary older
results, not outcomes of the new multibin extension.

\subsection{How far can teacher rankings be trusted?}
A new recomputation of the existing exact-geometry audit keeps DFT source,
phase, composition and cohort separate. Across seven historical O3 rattled
pools (524 frames), UMA median Spearman correlation is 0.97366, low-quintile
precision 96.30\%, and pair-gap root-mean-square error (RMSE) 2.047 meV/atom. Across ten informative Hu O3
optimized pools (104 frames), these medians are 0.73177, 41.67\%, and
4.077 meV/atom. Pool sizes/ties and all three models are tabulated separately.
Strongly displaced frames can give more favorable rank correlations than
nearly degenerate optimized structures.

Hu's optimized Li$_{10}$Co$_{12}$O$_{24}$ pool contains five structures. UMA picks
\texttt{structure\_4159} first although DFT ranks it fifth; its selection regret
is 7.9711 meV/atom relative to the best DFT member of that complete pool. Matching
all five identities is trivial top-five overlap here, not recall from a larger
pool. Historical/Hu DFT settings differ, and archived electronic/spin convergence
is not recertified. This audit used FP64; the new deletion benchmark uses its
separately pinned FP32 protocol. Good ranks do not establish accurate gaps,
forces, phonons, or thermal weights.

\subsection{Novelty, limits, and the DFT boundary}
The contribution is a reproducible test of composition/energy-range control,
strong baselines, symmetry-aware novelty/cost, and a measured acquisition layer.
It does not require the CVAE to win. It does not establish global minima,
DFT accuracy of new 48-site structures, physical entropy, full phase stability,
reactor optima, downstream MLIP improvement, or size/material transfer.
A later same-potential thermal pilot is kept separate from this class report.
Physical accuracy requires consistent matched DFT references; no new DFT is
submitted or scheduled in this work.
