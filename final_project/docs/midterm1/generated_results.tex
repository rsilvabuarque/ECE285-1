\subsection{Executed acquisition demonstration}
The three fits executed all 90 prespecified per-temperature and mixture
schedules: 11,520 attempts, 11,080 geometries, no failed requests, complete
two-teacher scoring. Under pure per-temperature requests, 100\% were asked to
be low, but measured low fractions were only 36.48\% UMA and 40.26\% MACE.
The floor requested 93.75/3.125/3.125\% after rounding; measured low fractions
were 35.79\% and 39.24\%. Correct budget execution did not overcome weak
conditional control.
The independent reference is fully scored: 24,576 raw draws collapse to
24,575 geometries. At 433.15 K its particle ESS is 4.629/2.271/5.480 for
$n=12/24/36$, with maximum representative mass 0.361/0.654/0.299.
Across the temperature grid, low-bin mass exceeds 99.986\%, so every rounded
128-attempt static request is 128/0/0 and the exploration request is 120/4/4.
Temperature changes weights/ESS, but not these coarse integer bin allocations.
Nested 512-to-8,192-draw prefixes find minimum-energy decreases of
0.0101/0.1758/0.2459 eV/cell. Thus stable bin fractions do not establish
adequate low-tail coverage or equilibrium accuracy.

The new campaign used 127,481 logical single-point energy/force/stress calls,
including 24 interface-pilot calls, and 12.401 allocated GPU-device hours
(3.100 four-GPU-equivalent node-hours). Both allocations are released. These
costs include startup, failures, analysis and idle time; inherited historical
labels are additional prior costs, not free end-to-end inputs. Neural-backbone
forward calls were not separately instrumented.
