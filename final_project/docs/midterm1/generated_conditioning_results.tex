\textbf{Development selects a spatial conditional sampler.}
Six dimension 8 fits tested balancing and both checkpoint rules on shared
trajectories; twelve more tested dimensions 4, 16, 32 and no-latent AR controls.
Balancing and selection weight 0.1 were retained; increasing latent size improved
reconstruction without monotonic discovery benefit. The frozen size rule retained
D4 using practical 1 percentage-point balanced and 2 point low-yield margins,
not confidence bounds. Graph-context development gave 60.88\% balanced yield
versus mutation's 58.80\%; its initial fit was replicated at two more seeds before
final generation. All 28 core fits completed 4000 updates, within the 30-fit cap.
Full policy/size tables and individual seeds are supplied separately.

\textbf{Better conditional sampling is not evidence of useful latent variables.}
Graph-context validation KL is only 0.007--0.011 nats; no pooled mean component
exceeds variance 0.01. Resampling $z$ at fixed uniforms changes 0.795 sites on
average and leaves 68.23\% in the same orbit. Posterior/prior Hamming distances
are 16.78/16.81. In contrast, dense D4 has KL 11.58--11.95 nats and changes
12.72 sites. Thus the positive result primarily supports a condition-sensitive
spatial AR sampler; the variational pathway is nearly unused by these diagnostics.
An explicit matched no-latent spatial control is a discriminating later test.

Symmetry-minimized Hamming distances to a fixed 1024-training-orbit reference per
count average 6.858 sites for context, 6.891 uniform and 6.498 mutation.
These are upper bounds on full-training nearest distance, not evidence of a large
diversity gain. All 3456 mutation attempts have their exact $2k$ edits verified.

\textbf{The second structural condition remains a negative pilot.}
One middle-energy fit requesting low/high vacancy-pair fractions gave 28.13\%
joint useful yield, versus 28.52\% plain CVAE and 44.79\% matched mutation.
The descriptor strongly correlates with teacher energy; it is not an independent
thermodynamic property. No multi-fit improvement is claimed. This separate pilot
and the paired conditional diagnostics are development evidence; the table is
the frozen final assessment.
