Learning data

training_original.npz contains the original Li12/24/36 subset (1536 orbits).
training_round0.npz contains that subset plus prior assessed configurations
promoted to adaptation data (50247 orbits). training_round1.npz additionally
contains25000 newly evaluated development configurations (75247 orbits).
Read the split field. Counts and input checksums are recorded in the preparation
and adaptation receipts and individual model configurations.

occupancy: N x48 binary uint8;1=Li present,0=vacancy, parent Li-site order.
n_Li: N integer; sum of occupancy, equal to12,24 or36 in this pilot.
energy_eV_atom: N real; UMA single-point energy divided by144+n_Li.
split: N strings train/val/test; grouped by canonical mask before fitting.
weight: N positive base weights; class balancing in round1 changes training
        weights inside the trainer, not the saved underlying energies.
structure_ids: N source identifiers. IDs are not numerical atom indices.
canonical_masks: N strings of48 vacancy bits;1=vacancy (opposite occupancy).
source: N strings identifying original or promoted assessment/development rows.

The parent data/parents/O3_N48_pilot.npz supplies numbers, positions_A, cell_A,
li_indices, and retained permutations. Li rows are48..95 in the192-row full
parent; never assume they are the first48. Coordinates are Angstrom, cell rows
are lattice vectors, atom count is144+n_Li. Absent Li must be removed when
exporting real structures; padding zeros are not atoms or force labels.

Proposal and scoring data

Development uses256 attempts per method/seed/composition; final uses512.
*_attempts.csv retains every attempt, including duplicates and known masks.
Canonical scoring IDs mask48_<12hex> identify vacancy-mask orbit keys.
*.npz contains all unique proposed masks; *_fresh.npz contains only keys outside
that pool's frozen history. Both retain the exact common pristine parent.

*_scores_uma.npz and *_scores_mace.npz contain all proposal IDs and total energy
in eV/cell, n_atoms, n_Li, and provenance=fresh/cached. New E/F/stress labels
are computed on canonical deletion geometries. Known-hit scalar energies are
reused from prior physically equivalent orientations. Known proposals consume
attempts and cannot contribute novel successes.

For fresh scores, force_component_rms_eV_A=sqrt(sum(F_a,alpha^2)/(3*n_atoms)),
force_max_eV_A=max_a norm(F_a), stress_eV_A3 has xx,yy,zz,yz,xz,xy order,
tensile positive. Cached rows deliberately have NaN in these extra force/stress
summaries; their finite energy is sufficient for the mask experiment. No missing
force/stress value enters training. Full freshly evaluated per-atom forces and
all raw shard evidence remain in the main HPC campaign scores/ directories;
that larger vector archive is not required for the collaborator mask package.

Interpretation

Training energy classes are calculated from that training set only. Main metric
thresholds remain the ORIGINAL per-teacher/count q20 in generation_thresholds.csv.
Different raw energies across teachers or Li counts are not directly pooled.
The final novelty boundary includes original, prior and all development masks.
Final is excluded from training and model selection. Future retraining on final
labels requires a new assessment pool and revised novelty cache.

These are MLIP-labeled deletion geometries. No new DFT, geometry minimization,
MD or thermodynamic validation was performed in this improvement cycle.
